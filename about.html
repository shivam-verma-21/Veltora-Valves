ABOUT VELTORA

Veltora Valve Pvt. Ltd.
Industrial Valve Manufacturer in Kathwada, Ahmedabad

Veltora Valve Pvt. Ltd. is a trusted industrial valve manufacturer and supplier based in Kathwada, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, delivering high-performance flow control solutions across India.

Established in 2026, we specialize in precision-engineered industrial valves, instrumentation valves, and automation systems designed for reliability, safety, and long-term performance.

Led by Mr. Hitesh Patel (20+ years) and Mr. Ashwin Sharma (10+ years), our company is built on engineering excellence, innovation, and customer-focused solutions.
