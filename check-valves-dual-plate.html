SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about VELTORA Dual Plate Check Valves, wafer and lug configurations, non-return operation and technical selection.

01 What is a Dual Plate Check Valve?

A Dual Plate Check Valve is a non-return valve that uses a paired-plate closure arrangement to permit flow in the intended direction and help prevent reverse flow. The exact internal design and closing characteristics depend on the selected model.

02 What is the difference between wafer-type and lug-type models?

The wafer-type model is designed for installation between matching pipeline flanges. The lug-type model has a lug-style body arrangement intended for compatible piping and flange layouts. Confirm body dimensions, fasteners and installation requirements from the specific product drawing.

03 How does a Dual Plate Check Valve help prevent backflow?

The valve's closure plates respond to flow conditions: they open for flow in the intended direction and close as flow reduces or reverses, helping restrict reverse flow. Closing response and leakage performance are design- and application-specific.

04 Which body and sealing materials are available?

The wider Check Valve range lists carbon steel, stainless steel, duplex and super duplex material families. Seat and sealing options include PTFE, RPTFE, PEEK, graphite and Stellite hard-faced seat. Exact body, plate, trim and seat combinations depend on the model and service conditions.

05 What pressure ratings and sizes are listed?

The supplied Check Valve portfolio lists ANSI Class 150 to 2500 and sizes from ½" (DN15) to 48" (DN1200). These are category-level ranges; confirm the exact pressure class and size combination available for the selected Dual Plate Check Valve model.

06 Does installation orientation matter?

Yes. Follow the flow-direction marking and the manufacturer's approved installation instructions. Whether horizontal or vertical installation is permitted depends on the selected valve design and model documentation.

07 How can I request pricing and technical specifications?

Share the required valve size, pressure rating, process medium, temperature, flow direction, installation orientation, end connection and material requirements with VELTORA. The team can discuss available configurations, technical documentation and quotation details.
