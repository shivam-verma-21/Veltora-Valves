SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about VELTORA One-Piece Ball Valves, material options, connections and selecting a suitable valve for your application.

01 What is a One-Piece Ball Valve?

A One-Piece Ball Valve features an integrated main body designed for industrial on-off flow control. Its compact construction can be considered where the selected valve's pressure, temperature and connection requirements match the application.

02 Which materials are available?

The VELTORA Ball Valve range includes carbon steel, stainless steel, forged steel, duplex and super duplex material options. The exact body, ball, stem, seat and sealing materials available for a One-Piece model must be confirmed against its approved product specification.

03 What connection options are offered?

The overall VELTORA Ball Valve range includes flanged, threaded, butt-weld and socket-weld connection options. Availability depends on the selected One-Piece model, size and approved product configuration. Please confirm the required connection before ordering.

04 How do I select the right valve?

Start by identifying the process fluid, operating pressure, temperature, required size, end connection and operating method. Material compatibility and applicable design and testing requirements should also be checked before final valve selection.

05 How can I request pricing and technical specifications?

Contact the VELTORA team with your required valve size, pressure class, material preference, connection type and application details. You can request a quotation and confirm the applicable technical specifications for your selected model.
