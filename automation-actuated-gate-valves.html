SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find helpful information about VELTORA actuated valve products, pneumatic and electric actuation, knife gate valve configurations, technical specifications and industrial applications. Confirm model-specific details with our team before ordering.

01 What are actuated gate valves?

Actuated gate valves combine a gate valve assembly with an actuator to provide automated opening and closing. Depending on the product, actuation may be pneumatic or electric. The actuator is selected according to the valve mechanism and process requirements.

02 What is an Electric Knife Edge Gate Valve?

An Electric Knife Edge Gate Valve combines a knife gate valve with an electric actuator for automated valve movement. It is intended for compatible industrial flow isolation applications. Actuator selection depends on the valve's operating mechanism and required operating force.

03 How does a Pneumatic Actuated Knife Gate Valve operate?

A Pneumatic Actuated Knife Gate Valve uses compressed air to operate its actuator. The actuator generates the movement required to open or close the knife gate. Required air supply, actuator configuration and operating arrangement must be verified for the selected model.

04 What is the difference between pneumatic and electric actuation?

Pneumatic actuators use compressed air to produce valve movement, while electric actuators use electrical input to operate the connected valve. The suitable arrangement depends on operating requirements, available utilities, installation environment and control system compatibility.

05 Which materials are available for actuated valves?

Available material families supplied in the automation portfolio include Carbon Steel, Stainless Steel, Forged Steel, Duplex Stainless Steel, Super Duplex Stainless Steel, Monel, Inconel, Hastelloy, Titanium, Alloy 20 and other exotic alloys on request. Material availability depends on the selected product and service conditions.

06 Which actuator options are available?

The supplied automation range includes Pneumatic Double Acting Actuators, Pneumatic Spring Return Actuators, Electric Multi-Turn Actuators and Electric Quarter-Turn Actuators. The correct actuator type depends on the connected valve mechanism and the required movement.

07 What pressure ratings and sizes are available?

The general automation portfolio includes PN10, PN16, PN25, PN40, PN64 and ANSI Class 150 through Class 2500 pressure ranges, with a general size range from ½ inch (DN15) to 48 inches (DN1200). These are not universal ratings for every product. Confirm actual sizes and ratings against the selected valve datasheet.

08 Which design and testing standards are applicable?

The supplied technical information references ASME B16.34, API 6D for applicable valve types, API 598, ISO 5208, MSS SP Series, IEC 60534 for control valve applications and ISO 5211 for actuator mounting where applicable. The final standards depend on the product design and project specification.

09 What inspection and testing can be performed?

Inspection may include Hydrostatic Shell Testing, Seat Leakage Testing, Pneumatic Pressure Testing, Electric Actuator Functional Testing, Actuator Torque Verification, Open and Close Cycle Testing, position indicator and limit switch verification, Visual and Dimensional Inspection and Material Traceability Verification. PMI and additional non-destructive testing are available on request, subject to the agreed inspection requirements.

10 Is the Pneumatic Control Globe Valve also a gate valve?

No. A Pneumatic Control Globe Valve is a globe-type control valve, whereas a knife gate valve uses a moving gate mechanism for isolation. Both can use pneumatic actuation, but their valve designs and functions are different.

Share the required product name, valve size, pressure rating, process medium, temperature, end connection and actuator requirements with VELTORA. Our team can assist with the appropriate product selection, technical documentation and quotation enquiry.
