SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about VELTORA Lug Style Butterfly Valves, lever and gear operation, connection compatibility and technical selection.

01 What is a Lug Style Butterfly Valve?

A Lug Style Butterfly Valve has threaded or tapped lugs around its body intended to align with compatible piping flange arrangements. The exact lug pattern, fastening arrangement and whether the model is suitable for end-of-line service must be confirmed from its approved technical documentation.

02 What is the difference between lever-operated and gear-operated models?

The lever-operated model uses a manual handle for quarter-turn operation. The gear-operated model uses a geared mechanism to operate the valve manually. The appropriate operator depends on valve size, operating torque and the selected model's design.

03 Is the gear-operated product also called a U-Section Lug Body Butterfly Valve?

Yes. The supplied product naming also identifies the gear-operated glossy-blue model as a U-Section Lug Body Butterfly Valve (Glossy Blue Type). Confirm the exact body profile and lug dimensions against the product drawing.

04 Which body, disc and seat materials are available?

The wider Butterfly Valve range lists cast iron, ductile iron, carbon steel, stainless steel, duplex and super duplex body options. Disc options include stainless steel, duplex, aluminium bronze, Monel, Hastelloy, titanium and nickel alloy. Seat options include EPDM, NBR, FKM, PTFE, RPTFE, silicone, neoprene and metal seat. Availability depends on the selected model and service conditions.

05 What pressure ratings and sizes are listed?

The supplied Butterfly Valve range lists PN10, PN16, ANSI Class 150 and ANSI Class 300, with sizes from 2" (DN50) to 72" (DN1800). These are category-level ranges; confirm the exact rating and size combination for the selected lug model.

06 Can a lug style butterfly valve be used for end-of-line service?

End-of-line capability is design- and rating-specific. Do not assume every lug-style valve is suitable for this duty. Confirm the manufacturer's approved installation instructions, fastening arrangement and pressure limits for the exact model.

07 How can I request pricing and technical specifications?

Share the required valve size, pressure rating, process medium, operating temperature, body/disc/seat material, flange standard, operating method and end-of-line requirement with VELTORA. The team can discuss the available model configuration and quotation.
