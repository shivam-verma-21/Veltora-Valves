SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find helpful information about VELTORA Automation Valves, pneumatic valve configurations, actuator options, technical specifications, materials and industrial applications. Confirm model-specific details with our team before ordering.

01 What types of automation valves are available?

VELTORA's automation valve range includes 22 & 23 Way Pneumatic Control Valves, 2-Way Pneumatic Angle Seat Valves, Angle Type Control Valves (Pneumatic Actuated) and Pneumatic Diaphragm Control Valves. The suitable configuration depends on the intended process function and operating requirements.

02 How do pneumatic automation valves operate?

Pneumatic automation valves use compressed air to operate a compatible actuator mechanism. The actuator produces the movement required to open, close or regulate the connected valve according to its design and control arrangement.

03 What is the difference between single-acting and double-acting actuators?

A single-acting pneumatic actuator uses compressed air for movement in one direction and a spring-return mechanism for the opposite direction. A double-acting actuator uses compressed air for movement in both directions. Selection depends on the required operating arrangement and fail-action requirements.

04 Which materials are available for automation valves?

Available material options include Carbon Steel, Stainless Steel, Forged Steel, Duplex Stainless Steel, Super Duplex, Monel, Inconel, Hastelloy, Titanium, Alloy 20, Nickel alloys, Bronze and Aluminium Bronze. Special alloys may also be considered on request. Final availability depends on the selected valve model and service conditions.

05 Which seat and diaphragm materials are available?

Available seat materials include PTFE, RPTFE, PEEK, Graphite and Metal Seat. Diaphragm options include EPDM, PTFE, PTFE + EPDM, Viton (FKM), NBR, Silicone, Neoprene and Hypalon. Material selection should be based on process fluid compatibility and operating conditions.

06 What pressure ratings and sizes are available?

The general automation portfolio includes PN10, PN16, PN25, PN40 and PN64, along with ANSI Class 150 through Class 2500 pressure ranges. The general size range extends from ½ inch (DN15) to 48 inches (DN1200). These ranges are not universal for every product. Actual pressure ratings and sizes must be confirmed against the selected valve datasheet.

07 Which end connections are available?

Available connection options include Flanged RF and RTJ, Threaded NPT/BSP/BSPT, Butt Weld, Socket Weld, Wafer, Lug and Tri-Clamp connections. Actual connection availability depends on the selected valve type, size and project requirements.

08 Which standards are applicable to automation valves?

The supplied technical information references ASME B16.34, API 6D for applicable valve types, API 598, ISO 5208, MSS SP Series, IEC 60534 for control valve applications and ISO 5211 for actuator mounting where applicable. Final standards depend on the product design and project specification.

09 What inspection and testing can be performed?

Inspection may include Hydrostatic Shell Testing, Seat Leakage Testing, Pneumatic Pressure Testing, actuator functional verification, torque verification, open-close cycle testing, fail-safe spring-return checks, position indicator and limit switch verification, visual and dimensional inspection and material traceability. PMI and additional non-destructive testing can be discussed according to project requirements.

10 What information is required to request a quotation?

Share the required valve type, size, pressure rating, process medium, operating pressure and temperature, body and sealing materials, end connection, actuator configuration and control requirements. Providing these details helps the VELTORA team review your application and quotation requirements.

11 How can I request technical assistance?

You can contact VELTORA with your product selection, process details and technical requirements. Our team can review the available valve configurations, relevant specifications and application requirements to assist with your enquiry.
