SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find helpful information about VELTORA Electric Actuated Ball Valves, their operating principle, actuator options, material selection and industrial applications. Confirm model-specific technical details with our team before ordering.

01 What is an electric actuated ball valve?

An electric actuated ball valve combines an industrial ball valve with an electrically operated actuator. The actuator provides rotational movement to open or close the ball valve, enabling automated flow isolation and remote operation in compatible industrial systems.

02 How does an electric actuator operate a ball valve?

The electric actuator converts electrical input into mechanical rotational movement. This movement operates the ball valve through its quarter-turn mechanism, positioning the valve for the required open or closed state. Required actuator torque and operating duty depend on the selected valve design.

03 Can these valves be integrated with PLC, DCS and SCADA systems?

Electric actuated ball valves can be configured for integration with compatible PLC, DCS and SCADA control environments. The electrical supply, control signals, feedback arrangement and interface requirements should be confirmed for the selected actuator.

04 Which electric actuator options are available?

The supplied automation range includes electric quarter-turn actuator options for relevant valve configurations. Weatherproof, explosion-proof and intelligent smart actuator options may be considered according to project requirements and the intended installation environment.

05 Which body and seat materials are available?

Available body material families include Carbon Steel, Stainless Steel, Forged Steel, Duplex Stainless Steel, Super Duplex Stainless Steel and selected special alloys. Seat and sealing options include PTFE, RPTFE, PEEK, Graphite and Metal Seat configurations, subject to the selected ball valve design.

06 What pressure ratings and sizes are available?

The supplied automation valve portfolio includes pressure classes such as PN10, PN16, PN25, PN40, PN64 and ANSI Class 150 through Class 2500. The general portfolio size range is ½ inch (DN15) to 48 inches (DN1200). Actual availability depends on the specific ball valve model and its design.

07 Which end connection options are offered?

Depending on the selected valve construction, end connection options can include Flanged RF / RTJ, Threaded NPT / BSP / BSPT, Butt Weld and Socket Weld. Confirm the required connection type and applicable dimensions against the selected model datasheet.

08 Which design and testing standards are applicable?

Applicable references may include ASME B16.34, API 6D and ISO 17292 for relevant ball valve designs, along with API 598 and ISO 5208 for applicable inspection and testing requirements. ISO 5211 may apply to actuator mounting. The exact standards depend on the selected configuration.

09 What inspection and testing can be performed?

Inspection can include Hydrostatic Shell Testing, Seat Leakage Testing, Electric Actuator Functional Testing, Actuator Torque Verification, Open and Close Cycle Testing, Position Indicator and Limit Switch Verification, Visual and Dimensional Inspection and Material Traceability. PMI and additional NDT procedures can be arranged on request, subject to the agreed inspection requirements.

10 How can I request a quotation and technical specifications?

Share your required valve size, pressure rating, process medium, operating temperature, end connection, actuator torque and electrical control requirements with VELTORA. Our team can assist with product selection, technical documentation and quotation enquiries.
