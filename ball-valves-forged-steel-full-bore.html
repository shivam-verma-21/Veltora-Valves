SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about forged steel full bore ball valves, carbon steel flanged models, three-piece forged construction and selecting a suitable valve for your application.

01 What is a forged steel ball valve?

A forged steel ball valve is a ball valve whose pressure-containing forged components are manufactured from forged steel stock. The exact forging grade, construction and pressure rating must be verified from the selected model documentation.

02 What is the difference between the two listed products?

The product group lists a Carbon Steel Ball Valve with flanged ends and a separate 3 Piece Ball Valve identified for high-pressure forged service. Their construction, material grade and connection details should be checked independently.

03 Which body materials are listed for the range?

The wider VELTORA Ball Valve range lists carbon steel grades ASTM A216 WCB, WCC and LCB, as well as forged carbon steel ASTM A105 and LF2. Confirm which material and grade apply to the specific model before ordering.

04 What does full bore mean, and how do I verify it?

Full bore identifies a valve design intended to provide a flow passage corresponding closely to the nominal pipeline bore. The actual passage diameter and dimensional details should be checked on the approved drawing for the selected valve.

05 How can I request pricing and technical specifications?

Share the product name, required size, pressure class, process medium, temperature, body material, end connection and any project requirements with VELTORA. The team can then discuss the relevant model specification and quotation.
