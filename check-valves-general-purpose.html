SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about VELTORA General Purpose Check Valves, flanged and threaded connections, non-return operation and technical selection.

01What is a General Purpose Check Valve?

A check valve is an automatic non-return valve intended to allow flow in the designated direction and help prevent reverse flow. The closure design varies by product type and application.

02What is a Silent Check Valve (Flanged Type)?

It is a flanged check-valve configuration intended for automatic non-return service. The product's closing mechanism and actual noise or surge performance must be confirmed from the approved model documentation and system conditions.

03Which thread standards are available for the inline hex-body model?

The wider check-valve information lists NPT, BSP and BSPT threaded options. Confirm the actual thread form, nominal size and pressure rating for the specific inline model before ordering.

04How does a Lift Check Valve operate?

In a lift-type check design, the closure element lifts away from its seat under suitable forward-flow conditions and returns toward the seat as flow decreases or reverses. Permitted installation orientation and closing performance are model-specific.

05Which body and sealing materials are listed?

The wider Check Valve range lists carbon steel, stainless steel, forged carbon steel, forged stainless steel, duplex and super duplex material families. Seat and sealing options include PTFE, RPTFE, PEEK, graphite and Stellite hard-faced seat. Exact grades and combinations depend on the valve design.

06What pressure ratings and sizes are listed?

The supplied Check Valve portfolio lists ANSI Class 150 to 2500 and sizes from ½" (DN15) to 48" (DN1200). These are category-level ranges; confirm the exact size and pressure-class combination for the selected product.

07How can I request pricing and technical specifications?

Share the required valve type, size, pressure class, process medium, temperature, connection standard and material requirements with VELTORA. The team can discuss product availability, technical documentation and quotation details.
