SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about One-Piece Threaded Ball Valves, screwed connections, material selection and manual or pneumatic operating options.

01 What is a One-Piece Threaded Ball Valve?

A One-Piece Threaded Ball Valve combines a one-piece body arrangement with screwed or threaded end connections. It is selected for compatible piping systems after confirming the required thread form and operating limits.

02 Which threaded connection options are available?

The wider VELTORA Ball Valve range lists NPT, BSP and BSPT threaded options. The exact thread form and size available for each One-Piece model should be confirmed and matched with the piping specification before ordering.

03 Which materials are available?

The broader Ball Valve range lists carbon steel, stainless steel, forged carbon steel, forged stainless steel, duplex and super duplex options. The supplied manual product image is marked A105. Exact material availability for each model and component must be confirmed against its datasheet.

04 Are manual and pneumatic actuated versions available?

The listed One-Piece Threaded product options include a manual screwed ball valve and a pneumatic actuated threaded ball valve. Actuator type, air supply, accessories and body details should be confirmed for the pneumatic model.

05 How can I request pricing and technical specifications?

Contact the VELTORA team with the required size, pressure class, process fluid, thread form, material preference and operating method. You can request a quotation and confirm the relevant specifications for the selected model.
