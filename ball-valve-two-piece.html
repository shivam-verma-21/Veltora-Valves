SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about VELTORA Two-Piece Ball Valves, product configurations, material options and selecting a suitable valve for your application.

01 What is a Two-Piece Ball Valve?

A Two-Piece Ball Valve generally consists of two principal body sections assembled together. The body-joint arrangement and other design details depend on the selected product model and its approved technical documentation.

02 Which materials are available?

The VELTORA Ball Valve range includes carbon steel, stainless steel, forged steel, duplex and super duplex material options. The exact body, ball, stem, seat and sealing materials available for a Two-Piece model must be confirmed against its approved product specification.

03 What pressure classes and sizes are available?

The overall Ball Valve range covers multiple pressure classes from Class 150 to Class 2500 and sizes from ½ inch (DN15) to 24 inches (DN600). The available size and pressure combinations must be confirmed for the selected Two-Piece model.

04 What end connection options are offered?

The overall VELTORA Ball Valve range lists flanged (RF / RTJ), threaded (NPT / BSP / BSPT), butt-weld and socket-weld options. Confirm which connection types are available for the selected Two-Piece model.

05 How do I select the right valve?

Start by identifying the process fluid, operating pressure, temperature, required size, end connection and operating method. Material compatibility and applicable design and testing requirements should also be checked before final valve selection.

06 How can I request pricing and technical specifications?

Contact the VELTORA team with your required valve size, pressure class, material preference, connection type and application details. You can request a quotation and confirm the applicable technical specifications for your selected model.
