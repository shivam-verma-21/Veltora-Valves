TECHNICAL SUPPORT

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about VELTORA offset disc butterfly valve configurations, connections, materials and technical selection.

01 What is an offset disc butterfly valve?

An offset disc butterfly valve uses an offset arrangement between the disc, shaft and/or seat geometry. The exact offset design and seat interaction depend on the selected valve model. The listed range includes a triple offset flanged model.

02 What is the difference between lug type and triple offset flanged models?

The listed lug type model uses a lug-style connection arrangement. The triple offset model is identified by its triple-offset disc design and flanged connection. Confirm the individual product drawings, dimensions and installation requirements before selection.

03 Which body, disc and seat materials are available?

The wider Butterfly Valve range lists cast iron, ductile iron, carbon steel, stainless steel, duplex and super duplex body options. Disc options include stainless steel, duplex, aluminium bronze, Monel, Hastelloy, titanium and nickel alloy. Seat options include EPDM, NBR, FKM, PTFE, RPTFE, silicone, neoprene and metal seat for high-temperature applications. Confirm model-specific availability and media compatibility.

04 What pressure ratings and sizes are listed?

The supplied Butterfly Valve range lists PN10, PN16, ANSI Class 150 and ANSI Class 300, with a size range from 2" (DN50) to 72" (DN1800). These are category-level ranges; verify the exact size and rating combination for the selected model.

05 Which flange standards are compatible?

The supplied range lists ANSI, DIN, BS and JIS flange compatibility. Confirm drilling, facing, dimensions and the applicable connection standard for the selected valve before ordering.

06 How can I request pricing and technical specifications?

Share the required valve type, size, pressure class, process medium, operating temperature, body and disc materials, seat preference, connection type and gear operator requirements with VELTORA. The team can discuss the relevant model specification and quotation.
