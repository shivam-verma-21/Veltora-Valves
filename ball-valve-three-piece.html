SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about VELTORA Three-Piece Ball Valves, construction, materials, operating options and product selection.

01 What is a Three-Piece Ball Valve?

A Three-Piece Ball Valve generally consists of a central body section assembled with two end pieces. The joint arrangement, service access and permitted maintenance method depend on the manufacturer's specific design.

02 Which materials are available?

The wider VELTORA Ball Valve range lists carbon steel, stainless steel, forged carbon steel, forged stainless steel, duplex and super duplex material options. The exact body, ball, stem, seat and seal grades available for a particular Three-Piece model must be confirmed against its approved product specification.

03 What connection options are available?

The overall Ball Valve range lists flanged (RF / RTJ), threaded (NPT / BSP / BSPT), butt-weld and socket-weld ends. The listed Three-Piece product includes flanged configurations. Confirm the exact connection and facing for the selected model before ordering.

04 Are manual and pneumatic versions available?

The listed Three-Piece products include a lever-operated flanged ball valve and a pneumatic actuated three-piece ball valve. Actuator type, air-supply requirements and control accessories should be confirmed for the selected pneumatic model.

05 How can I request pricing and technical specifications?

Contact the VELTORA team with your required size, pressure class, process fluid, material preference, end connection and operating method. You can request a quotation and confirm the applicable technical documentation for the selected Three-Piece model.
