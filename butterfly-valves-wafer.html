SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about VELTORA wafer butterfly valves, pneumatic and manual operation, materials, connections and technical selection.

01 What is a wafer butterfly valve?

A wafer butterfly valve has a compact body designed to be installed between compatible mating pipeline flanges. Confirm the valve pattern, flange drilling, face-to-face dimension, fasteners and installation instructions for the selected model.

02 What does spring-return pneumatic actuation mean?

A spring-return actuator uses compressed air to drive one direction of travel and spring force to return the actuator. Whether the valve is fail-open or fail-closed depends on the actuator and valve arrangement and must be confirmed from the approved specification.

03 What is the difference between gear-operated and lever-operated butterfly valves?

A lever-operated valve uses a direct manual handle, while a gear-operated valve uses a geared mechanism to operate the valve. The appropriate arrangement depends on valve size, required operating torque and the selected product design.

04 Which body, disc and seat materials are available?

The wider Butterfly Valve range lists cast iron, ductile iron, carbon steel, stainless steel, duplex and super duplex body options. Disc options include stainless steel, duplex, aluminium bronze, Monel, Hastelloy, titanium and nickel alloy. Seat options include EPDM, NBR, FKM, PTFE, RPTFE, silicone, neoprene and metal seat. Confirm material availability and media compatibility for the exact model.

05 What pressure ratings and sizes are listed?

The supplied Butterfly Valve portfolio lists PN10, PN16, ANSI Class 150 and ANSI Class 300, with sizes from 2 inches (DN50) to 72 inches (DN1800). These are portfolio-level ranges; verify the exact pressure and size combination for the selected valve.

06 Which design and testing standards are listed?

The supplied range lists API 609, MSS SP-67, EN 593, ASME B16.34, ISO 5752 and BS 5155 as design standards. Testing references include API 598, ISO 5208, EN 12266-1 and MSS SP-61. Confirm which standards apply to the ordered model and inspection plan.

07 How can I request pricing and technical specifications?

Share the required valve type, size, pressure rating, process medium, operating temperature, body/disc/seat material, flange standard, shut-off requirement and operator details with VELTORA. The team can discuss the relevant model specification and quotation.
