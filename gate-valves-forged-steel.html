Knife Gate Valve

The VELTORA Knife Gate Valve is a gate-type isolation product intended for applications where a sliding knife-shaped gate is used to interrupt flow. Knife gate valves are commonly evaluated for process duties involving slurries, suspended solids or viscous media, but suitability depends on the actual body, gate, seat and sealing design. Confirm whether the selected model is unidirectional or bidirectional, its end connection, pressure limits, material grades and installation requirements before specifying it.

PRODUCT 03 / ELECTRIC ACTUATED GATE VALVE

Motorized Electric Actuated Gate Valve

The VELTORA Motorized Electric Actuated Gate Valve combines a gate valve with an electric actuator for powered opening and closing. It can be considered for remote or automated isolation where the actuator supply, control signal, torque or thrust requirement, duty cycle and environmental protection are compatible with the installation. The actuator does not change the gate valve's basic isolation function. Confirm actuator make, voltage, control mode, enclosure protection, fail-position requirements and valve operating limits from the approved datasheet.

SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about VELTORA Forged Steel Gate Valves, end connections, shut-off operation and technical selection.

01Which products are included in this collection?

The supplied list includes a Knife Gate Valve and a Motorized Electric Actuated Gate Valve. It also lists a High Pressure Needle Valve (Threaded End), which is a separate valve type rather than a gate valve.

02What is the difference between a gate valve and a globe valve?

A gate valve is generally selected for on-off isolation, while a globe valve is commonly used for flow regulation. The supplied product list includes one flanged globe valve alongside two gate valve products.

03Which end connections are listed for VELTORA gate valves?

The supplied Gate Valve range lists flanged RF/RTJ, butt weld, socket weld and threaded NPT/BSP/BSPT options. The supplied needle valve is specifically identified as threaded end; confirm each other model connection separately.

04What is a bolted bonnet?

A bolted bonnet is a bonnet-to-body arrangement secured with bolting. Confirm the pressure class, gasket arrangement and maintenance procedure for the selected valve.

05Which body and trim materials are listed?

The supplied Gate Valve range lists carbon steel, stainless steel, forged carbon steel, forged stainless steel, duplex, super duplex, Monel, Inconel, Hastelloy, titanium, Alloy 20, nickel, bronze and aluminium bronze. Exact grades and combinations depend on the valve design.

06What pressure ratings and sizes are listed?

The supplied Gate Valve portfolio lists ANSI Class 150 to 2500 and sizes from ½" (DN15) to 48" (DN1200). These are category-level ranges; confirm the exact size and pressure-class combination for the selected product.

07How can I request pricing and technical specifications?

Share the required valve type, size, pressure class, process medium, temperature, connection standard and material requirements with VELTORA. The team can discuss product availability, technical documentation and quotation details.
