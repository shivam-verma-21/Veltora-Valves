SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Double Flanged and Wafer Type Butterfly Valves, flange compatibility, materials and technical selection.

01What is a Double Flanged Butterfly Valve?

A Double Flanged Butterfly Valve has flanged ends on both sides of the valve body for connection to compatible pipeline flanges. Confirm flange drilling, facing, pressure class and face-to-face dimensions for the selected model before installation.

02What is a Wafer Type Butterfly Valve?

A Wafer Type Butterfly Valve is designed to be installed between two mating pipe flanges. Its fitment depends on the valve dimensions, flange standard, bolting arrangement and the manufacturer's installation instructions.

03What is the difference between double-flanged and wafer-type models?

A double-flanged model has flanged ends as part of the valve body, while a wafer-type model fits between separate mating pipeline flanges. Their installation dimensions, bolting and flange compatibility differ, so check the appropriate model drawing.

04Which body, disc and seat materials are available?

The wider VELTORA Butterfly Valve range lists cast iron, ductile iron, carbon steel, stainless steel, duplex and super duplex body options. Disc options include stainless steel, duplex, aluminium bronze, Monel, Hastelloy, titanium and nickel alloy. Seat options include EPDM, NBR, FKM, PTFE, RPTFE, silicone, neoprene and metal seat. Availability depends on the selected model and service conditions.

05What pressure ratings and sizes are listed?

The supplied Butterfly Valve range lists PN10, PN16, ANSI Class 150 and ANSI Class 300, with sizes from 2" (DN50) to 72" (DN1800). These are category-level ranges; confirm the exact rating and size combination for the selected model.

06Which flange standards are compatible?

The supplied Butterfly Valve range lists ANSI, DIN, BS and JIS flange compatibility. Confirm the exact drilling, facing, bolt pattern and face-to-face dimensions for the selected double-flanged or wafer-type model.

07How can I request pricing and technical specifications?

Share the required valve type, size, pressure rating, process medium, operating temperature, flange standard, body/disc/seat material and operator arrangement with VELTORA. The team can discuss the applicable model configuration and quotation.
