04 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Glass-Lined Product FAQs

Answers to common questions about sight flow indicators, glass lining, materials and technical selection.

Which products are included in this range?

The supplied product range includes a Tubular Sight Flow Indicator and a Double Window Sight Flow Indicator. Model-specific details should be confirmed with the approved product documentation.

What is the purpose of a sight flow indicator?

A sight flow indicator provides a viewing arrangement for observing process flow in a compatible line. The required viewing style and construction should be selected for the service conditions.

What glass lining material is listed?

The supplied portfolio information lists high-silica borosilicate glass lining and spark-tested glass coating for corrosion protection. Confirm the glass specification for the selected product.

Which body materials are available?

The supplied information lists carbon steel (glass-lined) and stainless steel (application specific). Exact material availability depends on the selected product configuration.

Which trim and sealing materials are listed?

Listed trim options include SS304, SS316, SS316L, Hastelloy C22/C276, Monel Alloy 400 and Titanium Grade 2. Seat and seal options include PTFE, RPTFE, PEEK, Graphite, Viton (FKM) and EPDM.

What pressure classes and sizes are listed?

The portfolio information lists PN10, PN16, ANSI Class 150 and ANSI Class 300, application dependent, with a general size range of 1 inch (DN25) to 12 inches (DN300). Confirm actual limits for the selected model.

Which end connections are available?

The supplied range lists flanged end (RF), customized flanged connections and special process connections on request. Confirm flange dimensions and connection details for the selected product.

What inspection and testing can be performed?

Listed inspection includes glass lining spark test, hydrostatic shell test, seat leakage test, visual and dimensional inspection, glass thickness verification and material traceability verification. PMI, UT, RT, MPI and DPT are listed on request.

How can I request a quotation?

Contact VELTORA with the product name, process medium, operating pressure and temperature, size, connection details and any material or inspection requirements. The team can review the enquiry against the available product information.
