SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about VELTORA PTFE Seated Butterfly Valves, pneumatic actuation, materials and technical selection.

01 What is a PTFE Seated Butterfly Valve?

It is a butterfly valve configuration that uses a PTFE seat where specified. PTFE may be considered for selected services requiring chemical compatibility and sealing performance, subject to the actual fluid, concentration, temperature, pressure and complete valve materials.

02 What is a Pneumatic Actuated Wafer Type Butterfly Valve?

It combines a wafer-style butterfly valve body, intended for installation between matching flanges, with a pneumatic actuator for air-operated valve movement. Confirm wafer dimensions, flange compatibility, actuator arrangement and seat material for the selected model.

03 What does double-acting pneumatic actuation mean?

A double-acting pneumatic actuator uses compressed air for both opening and closing strokes. Actuator torque, supply pressure, control accessories and operating sequence should be confirmed from the approved actuator datasheet.

04 Is PTFE seating suitable for every corrosive chemical?

No universal chemical compatibility should be assumed. Suitability depends on chemical identity and concentration, temperature, pressure, permeation and the compatibility of all wetted components. Confirm the application with the material and product datasheets.

05 Which body, disc and seat materials are available?

The wider Butterfly Valve range lists cast iron, ductile iron, carbon steel, stainless steel, duplex and super duplex body options. Disc options include stainless steel, duplex, aluminium bronze, Monel, Hastelloy, titanium and nickel alloy. Seat options include PTFE, RPTFE, EPDM, NBR, FKM, silicone, neoprene and metal seat. Confirm model-specific availability and compatibility.

06 What pressure ratings and sizes are listed?

The supplied overall Butterfly Valve range lists PN10, PN16, ANSI Class 150 and ANSI Class 300, with sizes from 2" (DN50) to 72" (DN1800). These are category-level ranges; verify the exact size and rating combination for the selected PTFE seated model.

07 How can I request pricing and technical specifications?

Share the required valve size, pressure, process medium and concentration, operating temperature, body/disc/seat materials, end connection, actuator type and air-supply requirements with VELTORA. The team can discuss the available model configuration and quotation.
