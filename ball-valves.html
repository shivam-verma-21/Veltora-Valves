04 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Industrial Ball Valve FAQs

Answers to common questions about VELTORA ball valve designs, materials, connections and technical selection.

What types of ball valves are listed in VELTORA's product range?

The supplied product information lists one-piece, two-piece and three-piece flanged-end ball valves; one-piece, two-piece and three-piece threaded ball valves; three-way ball valves; jacketed ball valves; and three-piece forged-steel full-bore ball valves. Confirm the required model and configuration with the company.

Which materials are available for industrial ball valves?

The supplied material range includes carbon steel, stainless steel, forged steel, duplex and super duplex stainless steel, Monel, Inconel, Hastelloy, titanium, Alloy 20, nickel, bronze and aluminium bronze. Exact grades depend on the selected valve design and confirmed availability.

What pressure classes and sizes are listed?

The supplied product information lists pressure classes 150, 300, 600, 900, 1500 and 2500, with a stated size range of ½ inch (DN15) to 24 inches (DN600). Available combinations depend on valve design, material and pressure-temperature limits.

Which end connections can I enquire about?

The supplied information lists flanged ends (RF / RTJ), threaded ends (NPT / BSP / BSPT), butt-weld ends, socket-weld ends and customized connections. Please specify your piping connection requirements when requesting a quote.

Which standards and tests are mentioned for ball valves?

The supplied information lists API 6D, API 608, ASME B16.34, ISO 17292 and BS 5351 as design standards. API 598, API 6D and ISO 5208 are listed for testing. The applicable standard and required inspection documents should be confirmed for the specific valve being ordered.

Can I request a specific seat or sealing material?

The supplied range lists seat options including PTFE, RPTFE, PEEK, Devlon, Nylon, Graphite, metal seat, carbon-filled PTFE and glass-filled PTFE. Listed sealing and packing options include PTFE, graphite, FKM, EPDM, NBR and HNBR, with Kalrez® noted on request. Confirm suitability and availability for your application.

How do I select a suitable ball valve for my application?

Share the process media, operating pressure, temperature, line size, end connection, material requirements and operation type. These details help determine which available valve configuration may be suitable for your service conditions.

How can I request a quotation from VELTORA?

Contact VELTORA through the website enquiry page and share your valve type, size, pressure class, material, connection and quantity requirements. Include any applicable specifications or documentation requirements to help the team review your enquiry.
