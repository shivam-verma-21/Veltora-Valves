FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Industrial Automation Valve FAQs

Common questions about valve types, actuation and product selection.

What types of automation valves are available?

This category links to automated ball, gate, check and butterfly valve pages. Availability and configuration should be confirmed for the required model.

What is the difference between pneumatic and electric actuation?

Pneumatic actuators use a compressed-air supply, while electric actuators use electrical power. Selection depends on valve requirements, available utilities, control needs and site conditions.

Can an actuator be selected for an existing valve?

Compatibility must be checked using the valve interface, operating torque or thrust, duty cycle, available supply and required accessories.

Which specifications are needed for a quotation?

Share the valve type, size, pressure rating, materials, end connection, process fluid, operating temperature and pressure, actuator preference and control requirements where known.

Are all actuator accessories available for every valve?

No. Accessory compatibility depends on the actuator and valve configuration. Confirm availability for the specific assembly.

How do I confirm applicable standards and testing?

Refer to the specific product datasheet and purchase specification. Required standards, inspection and testing should be confirmed before order placement.

How can I request a quotation?

Use the contact page to share your application details and required configuration. The VELTORA team can review the information and advise on next steps.
