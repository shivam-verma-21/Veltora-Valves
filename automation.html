04 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Industrial Automation Valve FAQs

Common questions about actuation options, selection and industrial applications.

What are industrial automation valves?

Automation valve assemblies combine a compatible valve with an actuator to support operation without relying solely on manual handling. The correct configuration depends on valve type, process conditions and control requirements.

Which automation valve categories are listed on this page?

This page groups the range into Automated Ball Valves, Automated Gate Valves, Automated Check Valves and Automated Butterfly Valves. Confirm the actual available model and actuation arrangement with VELTORA.

What is the difference between pneumatic and electric actuation?

Pneumatic actuators use compressed air, while electric actuators use electrical power. Selection depends on available utilities, valve torque or thrust, operating frequency, control needs and site conditions.

Can an automation valve be integrated with PLC, DCS or SCADA?

Integration may be possible when the selected actuator and accessories support the required signals and interfaces. Confirm control method, feedback, power supply and communication requirements before ordering.

Are spring-return or fail-safe options available?

Pneumatic spring-return arrangements may be considered where offered. The required fail position and safety response must be specified and verified for the selected valve-actuator assembly.

What information should I provide for a quotation?

Share valve type, size, material, pressure class, end connection, process medium, operating pressure and temperature, actuator type, air or electrical supply, control accessories and applicable standards.
