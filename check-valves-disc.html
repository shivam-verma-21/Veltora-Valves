SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about VELTORA Disc Check Valves, single-disc and dual-plate configurations, wafer installation and technical selection.

01What is a Disc Check Valve?

A Disc Check Valve is an automatic non-return valve that uses a disc or plate closure element to permit flow in the intended direction and help restrict reverse flow. Internal design varies by model.

02What is the difference between single-disc and dual-plate check valves?

A single-disc model uses one closure disc, while a dual-plate model uses a paired-plate arrangement. Opening and closing behaviour, dimensions and installation limits depend on the selected design and its datasheet.

03What is a single-disc lift-type check valve?

It is a check-valve configuration in which a single closure element moves in a lifting direction in response to flow conditions. Confirm the exact lift mechanism, minimum flow requirements and permitted installation position from the approved model documentation.

04Which body and sealing materials are available?

The wider Check Valve portfolio lists carbon steel, stainless steel, forged carbon steel, forged stainless steel, duplex and super duplex material families. Seat and sealing options include PTFE, RPTFE, PEEK, graphite and Stellite hard-faced seat. Exact applicability to each disc/wafer model must be confirmed.

05What pressure ratings and sizes are listed?

The supplied Check Valve portfolio lists ANSI Class 150 to 2500 and sizes from ½" (DN15) to 48" (DN1200). These are category-level ranges; confirm the exact size and rating available for the selected wafer model.

06Does installation orientation matter?

Yes. Permitted horizontal or vertical installation depends on the selected check-valve design. Follow the flow-direction marking and manufacturer's approved installation instructions; do not assume all wafer check valves support every orientation.

07How can I request pricing and technical specifications?

Share the required valve size, pressure rating, process medium, temperature, flow conditions, installation position, end connection and material requirements with VELTORA. The team can discuss available configurations, technical documentation and quotation details.
