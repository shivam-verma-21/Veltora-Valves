SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Three-Way Flanged Ball Valves, L-Port and T-Port arrangements, materials and manual or pneumatic actuation.

01 What is a Three-Way Ball Valve?

A Three-Way Ball Valve has three ports and is selected for compatible flow routing or distribution requirements. The actual flow paths depend on the internal ball porting, valve position and selected model.

02 What is the difference between L-Port and T-Port?

L-Port and T-Port refer to different internal ball porting arrangements and therefore provide different flow-routing options. The connected ports at each handle or actuator position must be checked using the manufacturer's flow diagram for the exact valve model.

03 Are manual and pneumatic actuated versions available?

The listed product options include a manual three-way flanged ball valve and a pneumatic actuated three-way flanged ball valve. Actuator type, air supply, accessories and the selected port arrangement should be confirmed for the required model.

04 Which materials and pressure classes are available?

The wider VELTORA Ball Valve range lists carbon steel, stainless steel, forged steel, duplex and super duplex material options, with multiple pressure classes from Class 150 to Class 2500. Availability for a specific three-way model, size and material combination must be confirmed against its datasheet.

05 How can I request a quotation or technical flow diagram?

Share the required L-Port or T-Port configuration, size, pressure class, process medium, flange details and preferred operation method with VELTORA. You can request pricing, model specifications and the applicable flow or engineering diagram.
