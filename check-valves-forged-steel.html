SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about VELTORA Forged Steel Check Valves, flanged and threaded connections, materials and technical selection.

01What is a forged steel check valve?

A forged steel check valve is a non-return valve intended to allow flow in the desired direction and help prevent reverse flow. Its suitability depends on the valve design, materials, pressure rating and operating conditions.

02What is the difference between flanged and threaded check valves?

The flanged model connects to compatible pipeline flanges, with facing and drilling such as RF or RTJ confirmed to the specification. The threaded model uses a screwed connection such as NPT, BSP or BSPT. Select the arrangement that matches the piping design.

03What does bolted cover mean on the threaded model?

The product is identified as a threaded check valve with a bolted cover arrangement. Cover construction, fasteners, gasket or sealing details and maintenance requirements should be confirmed from the approved product drawing.

04Which materials and seat options are available?

The supplied range lists forged carbon steel ASTM A105 and LF2, forged stainless grades, carbon and stainless cast grades, duplex and super duplex options. Seat and sealing options include PTFE, RPTFE, PEEK, graphite and Stellite hard-faced seats. Exact combinations depend on the selected model.

05What pressure ratings and sizes are listed?

The supplied Check Valve range lists ANSI Class 150 to 2500 and sizes from ½ inch (DN15) to 48 inches (DN1200). These are category-level ranges; confirm the exact size and pressure-class combination for the chosen product.

06Which standards and tests may apply?

The supplied range lists API 594, API 6D where applicable, BS 1868, ASME B16.34, MSS SP-71 and MSS SP-80 for threaded types. Testing standards include API 598, API 6D for applicable models, ISO 5208 and MSS SP-61. Confirm the standards applicable to the selected design.

07How can I request pricing and technical specifications?

Share the required valve connection, size, pressure class, process medium, operating temperature, forged body grade, seat preference and installation conditions with VELTORA. The team can discuss model availability, technical documentation and quotation.
