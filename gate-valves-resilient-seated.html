Non-Rising Stem (NRS) Resilient Seated Gate Valve

The VELTORA Non-Rising Stem (NRS) Resilient Seated Gate Valve combines a resilient-seated gate arrangement with a non-rising stem configuration. During operation, the stem rotates to move the gate while the stem does not travel vertically above the handwheel in the manner of a rising-stem design. This arrangement can be considered where installation height or clearance is a design consideration. 01What is a resilient seated gate valve?

It is a gate valve that uses a resilient sealing surface at the gate to achieve shut-off against the valve seating area. The exact seat material and performance limits depend on the selected model.

02What does NRS mean in a gate valve?

NRS stands for Non-Rising Stem. The stem rotates to operate the gate but does not move vertically above the valve in the way a rising stem does. Confirm the operating nut or handwheel arrangement for the offered model.
