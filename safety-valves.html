04 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Industrial Safety Valve FAQs

Answers to common questions about safety relief valves, pressure reducing valves, materials, standards and product selection.

What types of safety valves are available in this range?

The listed range includes a Lever Operated Safety Valve (Test Lever Type), a Direct Operated Self-Operated Pressure Reducing Valve (PRV), a Spring Loaded Safety Relief Valve (Flanged End) and a Threaded Safety Relief Valve (Compact Type).

What is the purpose of a safety relief valve?

A safety relief valve is selected to protect compatible equipment or systems from overpressure by releasing fluid when the specified set pressure is reached, subject to the valve design and applicable requirements.

What is the function of a pressure reducing valve (PRV)?

A pressure reducing valve regulates downstream pressure to a required level under suitable operating conditions. It performs a pressure regulation function and is not the same device as a safety relief valve.

Which body materials are listed?

The supplied material range includes carbon steel, stainless steel, forged carbon steel, forged stainless steel, duplex and super duplex stainless steel, Monel, Inconel, Hastelloy, titanium, Alloy 20, Nickel 200/201 and other exotic alloys on request. Exact grades depend on model availability and service requirements.

Which seat and trim materials are available?

Listed seat and trim choices include stainless steel, Stellite hard-faced trim, PTFE, RPTFE, PEEK, graphite and metal seat for high-temperature applications. Confirm the suitable material for the process fluid and operating conditions.

What pressure ratings and sizes are listed?

The portfolio information lists ANSI Class 150, 300, 600, 900, 1500 and 2500; PN16, PN25, PN40, PN64 and PN100; and a general size range of ½ inch (DN15) to 12 inches (DN300). The actual combination must be confirmed for the selected model.

Which end connections can I request?

The listed connection range includes flanged ends (RF/RTJ), threaded ends (NPT/BSP/BSPT), socket weld (SW), butt weld (BW) and customized end connections. Availability depends on the specific valve design.

Which standards and testing methods are referenced?

Design references include ASME Section VIII, API 520, API 521, API 526, ASME B16.34, ASME B16.5 and ISO 4126. Testing references include API 527, API 598, ISO 4126, ASME Section VIII and MSS SP-61. Confirm which requirements apply to the selected product and project.

What inspection and testing may be performed?

The supplied list includes hydrostatic shell testing, seat leakage testing, set pressure calibration, blowdown adjustment, pop testing, reseating pressure verification, pneumatic functional testing, visual and dimensional inspection, material traceability and PMI. RT, UT, MPI and DPT are noted as available on request.

What information is needed for a quotation?

Share the valve type, process fluid, operating and design pressure, required set pressure, temperature, required capacity, size, connection, material and applicable code or project requirements. For a PRV, also provide inlet and required downstream pressure conditions.
