SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Three-Piece Threaded Ball Valves, screwed connections, material selection and choosing a suitable valve configuration.

01 What is a Three-Piece Threaded Ball Valve?

A Three-Piece Threaded Ball Valve uses a body assembled from three principal sections and screwed / threaded end connections for compatible piping. Exact body details and available thread forms depend on the model.

02 Which threaded connection options are available?

The wider VELTORA Ball Valve range lists NPT, BSP and BSPT threaded options. The exact thread form and size must be confirmed for the selected screwed valve model and matched with the piping specification.

03 Which materials are available?

The wider Ball Valve range lists carbon steel, stainless steel, forged carbon steel, forged stainless steel, duplex and super duplex options. Body, ball, stem, seat and seal grades available for a particular threaded model must be confirmed against its approved datasheet.

04 What sizes and pressure classes are offered?

The Ball Valve family range is listed from ½" (DN15) to 24" (DN600) and across Class 150 to Class 2500. Confirm the available size and pressure-class combination for the selected Three-Piece Threaded model.

05 How can I request pricing and technical specifications?

Contact the VELTORA team with your required size, pressure class, process fluid, thread form, material preference and operating method. You can request a quotation and confirm the relevant technical documentation for the selected model.
