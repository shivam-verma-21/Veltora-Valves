SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about VELTORA Single Plate Wafer Swing Check Valves, wafer-style installation, non-return operation and technical selection.

01 What is a Single Plate Wafer Swing Check Valve?

It is an automatic non-return valve that uses a single movable disc within a wafer-style body. It is installed between compatible pipeline flanges and is intended to allow flow in the desired direction while helping restrict reverse flow.

02 Is Wafer Type Check Valve (Single Plate Disc) the same product name?

Yes. The supplied product list identifies Single Plate Wafer Swing Check Valve and Wafer Type Check Valve (Single Plate Disc) as alternate descriptions for this one product. The product gallery shows the two supplied views of the same valve.

03 How does the single plate disc operate?

The disc responds to forward-flow forces to open the passage and moves back toward the seat as flow reduces or reverses. Actual opening, closing response and pressure drop depend on the selected design and system conditions.

04 How is a wafer check valve installed?

A wafer-style body is fitted between compatible mating flanges. Confirm the flow-direction marking, face-to-face dimensions, flange standard, bolting arrangement and permitted installation orientation from the approved instructions.

05 Which body, trim and seat materials are listed?

The supplied Check Valve range lists carbon steel, stainless steel, forged carbon steel, forged stainless steel, duplex and super duplex options. Seat and sealing options include PTFE, RPTFE, PEEK, graphite and Stellite hard-faced seat. Exact availability must be confirmed for the selected model.

06 What pressure ratings and sizes are available?

The supplied wider Check Valve range lists ANSI Class 150 to 2500 and sizes from ½ inch (DN15) to 48 inches (DN1200). These are category-level ranges; confirm the actual wafer model size and pressure combination before selection.

07 How can I request pricing and technical specifications?

Share the required size, pressure class, flange standard, process medium, operating temperature, flow conditions and material preference with VELTORA. The team can discuss the model-specific drawing, available specifications and quotation.
