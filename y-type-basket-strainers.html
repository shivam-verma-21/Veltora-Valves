04 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Y-Type & Basket Strainer FAQs

Answers to common questions about strainer types, screen materials, connections and technical selection.

What is the purpose of an industrial strainer?

An industrial strainer removes unwanted solid particles and contaminants from a process stream to help protect downstream equipment such as pumps, valves, flow meters, compressors and heat exchangers. The suitable design and screen depend on the process and filtration duty.

What is the difference between Y-type and basket type strainers?

A Y-type strainer uses an angled chamber and screen arrangement, while basket strainers use a basket-style element within the body. T-type, pot-type and duplex basket arrangements have different layouts and maintenance considerations. Confirm the construction drawing for the selected model.

Which strainer configurations are available in this range?

The supplied product list includes a vertical T-type basket strainer, butt-weld Y-type strainer, duplex basket changeover strainer, flanged Y-type strainers with bolted or hinged covers, forged steel Y-type strainers, pot-type basket strainer and threaded or flanged Y-type strainers.

Which body materials are listed for the strainers?

The supplied material range lists carbon steel ASTM A216 WCB, WCC and LCB; stainless steel ASTM A351 CF8, CF8M, CF3 and CF3M; forged carbon steel ASTM A105 and LF2; forged stainless steel ASTM A182 F304, F304L, F316 and F316L; duplex and super duplex stainless steel grades. Confirm the exact grade for the selected product.

Which screen materials are available?

The supplied screen material options are Stainless Steel SS304, Stainless Steel SS316 and Duplex Stainless Steel. Screen mesh and filtration requirements should be confirmed for the process application.

What pressure ratings and sizes are listed?

The supplied portfolio range lists ANSI Class 150 to 2500, PN10 to PN40 and sizes from ½ inch (DN15) to 24 inches (DN600). These are general range statements; the available combination depends on the selected design and model.

Which end connections can I request?

The supplied information lists flanged ends RF / RTJ, threaded ends NPT / BSP / BSPT, butt weld ends BW and socket weld ends SW. Not every connection is necessarily available for every strainer design.

Which testing and inspection methods are listed?

Testing references include API 598, ISO 5208 and MSS SP-61. Inspection includes hydrostatic shell test, body integrity test, visual and dimensional inspection, material traceability verification, and screen inspection and mesh verification. PMI, UT, RT, MPI and DPT are listed on request.

How do I select the right strainer for my pipeline?

Share the process medium, operating pressure and temperature, line size, connection type, required screen material and mesh, contaminant characteristics, installation layout and maintenance access. These details help review a suitable strainer configuration.

How can I request a quotation?

Contact VELTORA through the enquiry page and provide the product name, size, pressure rating, body and screen materials, end connection, process conditions and quantity. Attach a specification or drawing if available.
