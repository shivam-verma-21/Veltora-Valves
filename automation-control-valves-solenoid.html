2-Way Normally Closed Pilot-Operated Diaphragm Solenoid Valve

The VELTORA 2-Way Normally Closed Pilot-Operated Diaphragm Solenoid Valve is designed for electrically controlled flow switching in compatible industrial systems. In its de-energized state, the normally closed arrangement is intended to keep the main flow path shut. When the coil is energized, the pilot mechanism changes the pressure conditions across the diaphragm and allows the main valve to open, provided the required operating differential pressure is available. Confirm the working medium, minimum and maximum differential pressure, coil voltage and frequency, diaphragm material, port size and temperature range against the approved model datasheet.

SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find helpful information about VELTORA Pneumatic Control Valves, their operating principles, material options, actuator requirements and industrial applications. Confirm model-specific technical details with our team before ordering.

01 What is a pneumatic control valve?

A pneumatic control valve uses compressed air to operate its actuator and regulate process flow. It is used in compatible industrial automation systems where controlled fluid flow and process regulation are required.

02 How does pneumatic actuation work?

Pneumatic actuation uses compressed air to generate mechanical movement in the actuator, which operates the connected valve. The actuator configuration and operating air pressure must be selected according to the required valve operation.

03 Which materials are available for pneumatic control valves?

Available body material options include Carbon Steel, Stainless Steel, Forged Carbon Steel, Forged Stainless Steel, Duplex Stainless Steel, Super Duplex Stainless Steel, Monel, Inconel, Hastelloy, Titanium, Alloy 20 and other exotic alloys on request. Actual material availability depends on the selected model.

04 Which trim and seat materials are offered?

The supplied material range includes Stainless Steel Trim, Hardened Stainless Steel, Stellite Hard-Faced Trim, PTFE, RPTFE, PEEK, Graphite and Metal Seat options for selected high-temperature applications. Confirm compatibility with the process medium and operating conditions.

05 What pressure ratings and sizes are available?

The supplied pneumatic control valve portfolio specifies ANSI Class 150 to ANSI Class 2500 and sizes from ½ inch (DN15) to 24 inches (DN600). Actual pressure and size availability depend on the selected valve design and approved product datasheet.

06 Which design standards are followed?

The supplied technical information references ASME B16.34, IEC 60534, ISA S75 Series, ANSI/FCI 70-2 and ISO 5211 for actuator mounting. The applicable standard depends on the selected valve configuration and its intended service.

07 What testing and inspection procedures are included?

The supplied inspection details include Hydrostatic Shell Testing, Seat Leakage Testing, Pneumatic Actuator Functional Testing, Stroke Calibration, Control Response Verification, Visual and Dimensional Inspection and Material Traceability Verification. PMI and additional NDT procedures are available on request, subject to the inspection requirements.
