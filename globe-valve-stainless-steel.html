SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find helpful information about VELTORA globe valve configurations, materials, end connections, design references and technical selection.

01What is a globe valve used for?

A globe valve is commonly selected for flow regulation and shut-off duties. Its suitability depends on the required flow characteristics, pressure drop, fluid properties, trim and operating conditions.

02What does OS&Y mean in a flanged globe valve?

OS&Y means Outside Screw and Yoke. It identifies the stem and yoke arrangement. The specific construction, dimensions and operating details should be confirmed from the selected valve drawing and datasheet.

03Which body and bonnet materials are listed?

The supplied range lists Carbon Steel ASTM A216 WCB/WCC/LCB; Stainless Steel ASTM A351 CF8/CF8M/CF3/CF3M; Forged Carbon Steel ASTM A105/LF2; Forged Stainless Steel ASTM A182 F304/F304L/F316/F316L; Duplex F51/F60; and Super Duplex F53/F55. Exact grade availability depends on the product model.

04Which seat and sealing materials are available?

The supplied information lists Stainless Steel Seat, Stellite Hard-Faced Seat, PTFE Seat for application-specific use, Graphite Packing, Expanded Graphite Packing and Spiral Wound Gasket. Confirm suitability for the process medium and operating temperature.

05What pressure classes and sizes are listed?

The supplied overall range lists ANSI Class 150 to 2500 and sizes from ½ inch (DN15) to 24 inches (DN600). These are range-level figures and are not a guarantee that every size and class combination is available for every globe valve model.

06Which end connections can be specified?

The supplied options include Flanged End RF/RTJ, Butt Weld End BW, Socket Weld End SW and Threaded End NPT/BSP/BSPT. Confirm connection availability for the required model and line specification.

07Which design and testing standards are referenced?

The supplied list references API 623, BS 1873 and ASME B16.34, with API 602 for forged globe valves where applicable. Testing references include API 598, ISO 5208 and MSS SP-61. Confirm applicability for the specific valve design and project specification.

08What information is needed for a quotation?

Share the product name, valve type, size, pressure class, process medium, operating pressure and temperature, body and trim material preference, end connection, quantity and any inspection or documentation requirements.
