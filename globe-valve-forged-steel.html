SUPPORT & TECHNICAL INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

Find helpful information about VELTORA globe valve types, forged construction, end connections, materials, standards and application selection.

01What types of globe valves are included in this range?

The listed products are Flanged Globe Valve (Industrial Heavy Duty), Forged Steel Globe Valve (Bolted Bonnet), Screwed Threaded Globe Valve and Angle Type Globe Valve. Confirm the exact model design and configuration with its product datasheet.

02Which body and bonnet materials are available?

The supplied material range lists Carbon Steel ASTM A216 WCB, WCC and LCB; Stainless Steel ASTM A351 CF8, CF8M, CF3 and CF3M; Forged Carbon Steel ASTM A105 and LF2; Forged Stainless Steel ASTM A182 F304, F304L, F316 and F316L; and Duplex / Super Duplex grades. Exact grades depend on the selected model.

03What is the difference between a flanged, threaded and angle type globe valve?

Flanged globe valves connect to compatible pipeline flanges. Screwed threaded models use a specified pipe-thread connection. Angle type globe valves use an angle-pattern flow path. The appropriate style depends on piping layout, flow direction, process conditions and the selected model details.

04Which design standards are listed for globe valves?

The supplied information lists API 623, BS 1873 and ASME B16.34, with API 602 for forged globe valves and ISO 17292 where applicable. Not every standard applies to every design. Confirm the standard relevant to the selected product.

05What pressure ratings and sizes are available?

The supplied overall range lists ANSI Class 150 to 2500 and sizes from ½ inch (DN15) to 24 inches (DN600). These are range-level figures and should not be treated as universal limits for every globe valve model. Confirm model-specific ratings and dimensions.

06Which seat and sealing materials can be specified?

The supplied options include Stainless Steel Seat, Stellite Hard-Faced Seat, PTFE Seat for application-specific use, Graphite Packing, Expanded Graphite Packing and Spiral Wound Gasket. Confirm compatibility and availability for the selected valve and process medium.

07What inspection and testing can be performed?

The supplied inspection list includes Hydrostatic Shell Test, Seat Leakage Test, Back Seat Test, Visual and Dimensional Inspection, Material Traceability Verification and PMI, RT, UT, MPI and DPT on request. API 598, ISO 5208 and MSS SP-61 are listed testing references. Agreed inspection scope must be confirmed before order placement.

08How can I request a quotation?

Share the globe valve type, size, pressure class, process medium, operating pressure and temperature, material preference, end connection and quantity. Include any project standard or inspection requirement so the team can review the enquiry.
